PhotoSelect 1.0.2 · on your own Mac

Field tests for your M2

Run these on your Mac with your own Nikon and Olympus files. Nothing needs uploading: fill in the boxes as you go, attach the diagnostic report the app creates, then press Copy results and paste the text into our chat. Your answers are kept in this browser as you type, so you can stop and come back.

1 · Get the appDownload PhotoSelect-AppleSilicon-dmg (v1.0.2) while signed in to GitHub. Unzip it, open the DMG and drag PhotoSelect to Applications.
2 · Run the testsDo them in order; T4 and T5 matter most. Mark each one Pass, Issue or Skipped. Skipping is fine, just say why.
3 · Report backHelp → Create Diagnostic Report… after T2 and T8. Copy the results below and paste them in chat, with the report files attached or pasted.

Results to paste back

Press Copy results, then paste into the chat. If copying is blocked, click into the box, select all and copy.
