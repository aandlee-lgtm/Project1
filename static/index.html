Recommendation thresholds

Suggestions only. Nothing is deleted or moved, and a frame is never marked Drop just because another frame in its burst scored higher.

Burst matching

Groups consecutive frames by capture time (with sub-seconds when the camera records them), a small grey-level thumbnail and average colour; each frame is compared with the previous one, so panning sequences stay together. Frames without capture times stay ungrouped.

Understand the scores

Scores are relative to this folder, not probabilities or absolute quality: even a folder of soft photos has a top frame.

Sharpness: edge detail across the frame. Subject focus: edge detail in the central region, or the region you draw, measured on the full-resolution RAW decode. Noise, waves, foliage and a sharp background can inflate both; a noise warning appears when this folder's noise estimate is high.

Composition: edge placement near thirds and border clutter. It is not a trained aesthetic model and cannot judge expressions, the decisive moment or a cropped rig. Exposure: share of unclipped pixels in the rendered image.

If a RAW file's pixels cannot be decoded (for example Nikon High Efficiency NEF) but the camera stored a full-size JPEG inside it, that JPEG is analysed instead and the photo is labelled CAMERA PREVIEW. Its scores include in-camera sharpening and noise reduction.

Not included: eye or face detection, subject recognition, motion-blur detection, artistic judgement.
